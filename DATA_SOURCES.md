# Data sources

Bundled manager histories, identities, terms and notes are fictional. Startup market inputs are synthetic fixtures, not cached public history. Public observations are retrieved only when requested.

## Inventory

| Source | Contents and use |
| --- | --- |
| [synthetic_managers.csv](data/synthetic_managers.csv) | 18 fictional managers; 1,010 source rows and 1,007 accepted monthly observations. Generated by `backend/app/demo.py`, seed 91. Used for imports, analytics, ranking and offline operation. |
| [demo_upload_fresh.csv](data/demo_upload_fresh.csv) | Three new IDs: DEMOHEDGE, DEMONEUTRAL and DEMOTREND. Copies of bundled Harbor, Vector and Orion synthetic histories: 180 valid observations, one duplicate and two invalid/conflicting rows. Confirming the import adds three managers; Load demo retains uploads. |
| Synthetic market fixtures | Seven benchmarks, three macro/yield series and six factors, generated by `CachedDemoProvider`, seed 417. Used for offline comparisons, risk-free proxies and factor/regime analysis. |
| Yahoo Finance via yfinance | Adjusted prices for SPY, QQQ, AGG, HYG, IWM, TLT and GLD. Used for monthly benchmark returns and relative-performance comparisons. |
| FRED | TB3MS, VIXCLS and DGS10. Used for a monthly risk-free proxy and descriptive volatility/rate regimes. |
| Kenneth French Data Library | Monthly Mkt-RF, SMB, HML, RMW, CMA and momentum. Used for explanatory factor regressions. |
| User CSV/XLSX imports | Manager observations validated through the same pipeline. Original files, digests, mappings, corrections and source rows are retained. |

Each market source carries its identifier, retrieval time, transformation and live/synthetic status. A partial refresh can mix live observations and synthetic fallbacks. Public datasets and local database volumes are not bundled in Git.

## Public-source transformations

**Yahoo:** the final adjusted close of each completed calendar month produces monthly price relatives. The current incomplete month is excluded. Symbols represent US large-cap equities (SPY), Nasdaq-100 (QQQ), aggregate bonds (AGG), high-yield bonds (HYG), small-cap equities (IWM), long-duration Treasuries (TLT) and gold (GLD). These are adjusted ETF return approximations. [Provider documentation](https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/reference/api/yfinance.download.html).

**FRED:** TB3MS, an annualized percentage Treasury-bill yield, is divided by 1,200 for a monthly risk-free proxy. This is not an exact holding-period return. VIXCLS and DGS10 become monthly means for descriptive regime comparisons. [TB3MS](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/TB3MS), [VIXCLS](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/VIXCLS), [DGS10](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10).

**French factors:** monthly five-factor and momentum files are joined by calendar month; percentages become decimals and missing-value sentinels are masked. Regressions require at least 24 matched observations and a full-rank matrix. Fund excess returns use the FRED proxy; Mkt-RF retains its published definition. [Five-factor methodology](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library/f-f_5_factors_2x3.html), [momentum methodology](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library/det_mom_factor.html).

## Derived records

Metrics, eligibility decisions, score contributions, rankings and memo claims are saved evaluation records, not independent source datasets. Evidence links calculations to input periods, methods and original rows. Word and Excel exports copy saved results.

Ollama and OpenAI are interpretation providers, not financial data sources. No real manager factsheets, holdings, client portfolios, EDHEC/Kaggle manager datasets, Bloomberg or Aladdin feeds are incorporated.
